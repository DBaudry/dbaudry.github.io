I am a researcher at Inria and Université Grenoble-Alpes, in the [GHOST team](https://ghost-team.gitlabpages.inria.fr/) in Grenoble, which I joined in July 2025.

My research focuses on the theory of reinforcement learning, with a particular interest in posterior sampling and policy gradient methods. I study the exploration and optimization properties of these methods to understand when and how efficiently they learn. My interests also include multi-armed bandits and online learning.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, working with [Patrick Rebeschini](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/~rebeschi/) on online learning and optimization. 

Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Inria Fairplay team, based at ENSAE Paris, collaborating with [Vianney Perchet](https://vianney.ai/). My work focused on online algorithms and bandit models, particularly in structured and constrained problem settings, with practical applications in online advertising and recommendation systems.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Lille, supervised by [Emilie Kaufmann](http://chercheurs.lille.inria.fr/ekaufman/) and [Odalric-Ambrym Maillard](http://odalricambrymmaillard.neowordpress.fr/) in the Inria ScooL team. My thesis focused on non-parametric algorithms for multi-armed bandits, with applications in agriculture and extensions to risk-aware learning and non-stationary environments. During my PhD, I also spent three months at Kyoto University collaborating with [Junya Honda](http://stat.sys.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/honda/index_e.html).
