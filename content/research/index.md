My research focuses on the theory of reinforcement learning and online learning. I study how algorithms gather information, optimize their decisions and adapt to the structure of a problem. The aim is to understand both what makes learning difficult and how to design algorithms with precise performance guarantees.

## Connections to applications

I also collaborate on agricultural applications, including [adaptive crop-management trials](/publication/fieldcrops/) and [analyses of coffee varietal performance across environments](/publication/arabica-climate-gradients/). These collaborations connect questions about learning and statistical inference to decisions made under environmental uncertainty.
