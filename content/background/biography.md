[Download my CV (PDF)](/files/CV%20Baudry.pdf)

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, working with [Patrick Rebeschini](https://www.stats.ox.ac.uk/~rebeschi/) on online learning and optimization.

Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Inria Fairplay team, based at ENSAE Paris, collaborating with [Vianney Perchet](https://vianney.ai/). My work focused on online algorithms and bandit models, particularly in structured and constrained problem settings, with practical applications in online advertising and recommendation systems.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Lille, supervised by [Emilie Kaufmann](https://chercheurs.lille.inria.fr/ekaufman/) and [Odalric-Ambrym Maillard](https://odalric-ambrymmaillard.github.io/) in the Inria ScooL team. My thesis focused on non-parametric algorithms for multi-armed bandits, with applications in agriculture and extensions to risk-aware learning and non-stationary environments. During my PhD, I also spent three months at Kyoto University collaborating with [Junya Honda](https://stat.sys.i.kyoto-u.ac.jp/honda/index.html).
