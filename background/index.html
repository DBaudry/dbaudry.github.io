Background & CV

Download my CV (PDF)

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, working with Patrick Rebeschini on online learning and optimization.

Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Inria Fairplay team, based at ENSAE Paris, collaborating with Vianney Perchet. My work focused on online algorithms and bandit models, particularly in structured and constrained problem settings, with practical applications in online advertising and recommendation systems.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Lille, supervised by Emilie Kaufmann and Odalric-Ambrym Maillard in the Inria ScooL team. My thesis focused on non-parametric algorithms for multi-armed bandits, with applications in agriculture and extensions to risk-aware learning and non-stationary environments. During my PhD, I also spent three months at Kyoto University collaborating with Junya Honda.

Experience

  1. Researcher

    Inria, Université Grenoble-Alpes

    Jul 2025 – Present · Grenoble, France

    Developing theoretical guarantees for reinforcement learning, with a focus on posterior sampling and policy gradient methods. Recent work covers instance-dependent regret in episodic MDPs and the bias of two-timescale stochastic approximation, alongside PhD and internship supervision in the GHOST team.

  2. Research visit

    Collaboration with Nicolò Cesa-Bianchi and Andrew Jacobsen

    Oct 2025 · Milan, Italy

    Visited Nicolò Cesa-Bianchi and worked with him and Andrew Jacobsen, then a postdoctoral researcher in his group, on bandit convex optimization. This collaboration led to a perturbation approach to unconstrained linear bandits (ICML 2026), connecting bandit feedback to online linear optimization.

  3. Post-doctoral researcher

    Department of Statistics, University of Oxford

    Jul 2024 – Jun 2025 · Oxford, United Kingdom

    With Patrick Rebeschini, shifted my focus toward policy gradient methods and online convex optimization. Characterized how learning rates affect regret in stochastic gradient bandits and developed guarantees for non-stationary bandit convex optimization.

  4. Post-doctoral researcher

    Inria Fairplay, ENSAE, Ecole Polytechnique

    Jan 2023 – Jun 2024 · Palaiseau, France

    With Vianney Perchet, extended my work to learning in structured and strategic settings, including bandits with revenue constraints, online auctions and optimal stopping. Also investigated the value of reward lookahead in reinforcement learning.

  5. Visiting student

    Kyoto University

    Apr 2022 – Jul 2022 · Kyoto, Japan

    Worked with Junya Honda on a unified regret analysis of randomized bandit algorithms, bringing Thompson sampling and Minimum Empirical Divergence under a common framework for parametric and non-parametric reward models. Research visit supported by the RELIANT associate team.

  6. Teaching Assistant

    University of Lille

    Jan 2020 – Apr 2022 · Lille, France

    Taught around 150 hours of courses and tutorials in data mining, natural language processing and mathematics for economics, at bachelor’s and master’s levels.

  7. Data Scientist

    Kayrros

    May 2019 – Oct 2019 · Paris, France

    Developed machine learning methods to detect structural changes in energy markets, combining financial time series, news and satellite-derived signals.
